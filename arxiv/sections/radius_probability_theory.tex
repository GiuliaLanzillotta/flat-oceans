\subsubsection{Probabilistic radius guarantees}
\label{app:radius-confidence}
Fix the data, reference parameters and a nonzero subspace $M$. Write
$D_v(t)=\|F_X(\theta_0+tv)-F_X(\theta_0)\|_X$ for the output change along a unit direction $v$. For an invariant subspace this equals $\|A_X^{\rm tr}(tv)\|_X$. The directional failure probability is
\[
 p(r,\varepsilon)=\Pr_{v\sim\operatorname{Unif}(S(M))}
 \left[\sup_{0\le t\le r}D_v(t)>\varepsilon\right].
\]
Thus $p(r,\varepsilon)\le\delta$ means that at least a fraction $1-\delta$ of directions preserve the outputs within tolerance along their entire segment of length $r$.

For fixed radii $0<r_1<\cdots<r_m$, let
\[
 Y_j(v)=\max_{h\le j}D_v(r_h),\qquad
 p_j(\varepsilon)=\Pr_v[Y_j(v)>\varepsilon].
\]
The grid probability satisfies $p_j(\varepsilon)\le p(r_j,\varepsilon)$. A statistical upper bound on $p_j$ therefore certifies the tested grid; extending it to the continuous ray requires a bound between grid points. For example, if $|D_v(t)-D_v(t')|\le L|t-t'|$ uniformly in $v$ and $\Delta$ is the largest grid spacing (including $r_0=0$), then
\[
 p(r_j,\varepsilon)\le p_j(\varepsilon-L\Delta/2)
 \qquad (\varepsilon\ge L\Delta/2).
\]
Indeed, every point lies within $\Delta/2$ of a tested point or the origin, where $D_v(0)=0$.

\begin{proposition}[Simultaneous directional certificates]
\label{prop:probabilistic-radius}
Consider $K$ fixed checkpoint--space--radius comparisons at a fixed tolerance. In each comparison, sample $n$ independent uniform directions and observe $k$ failures. For total confidence error $\alpha\in(0,1)$, put $\eta=\alpha/K$ and
\[
 \overline p(k,n)=
 \begin{cases}
 \operatorname{Beta}^{-1}(1-\eta;k+1,n-k),&k<n,\\
 1,&k=n.
 \end{cases}
\]
Here $\operatorname{Beta}^{-1}(q;a,b)$ is the $q$-quantile of the beta distribution with shape parameters $a,b$. With probability at least $1-\alpha$ over the sampled directions, every reported grid failure probability is at most its bound $\overline p$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For each comparison the indicators are independent Bernoulli variables with failure probability $p_j$, so their sum is binomial. For $k<n$, the stated bound solves
\[
 \Pr_{Z\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(n,\overline p)}[Z\le k]=\eta.
\]
Inverting this binomial test gives the one-sided Clopper--Pearson bound with coverage at least $1-\eta$ \citep{clopper1934confidence}. A union bound over the $K$ comparisons gives simultaneous coverage at least $1-K\eta=1-\alpha$. Independence between comparisons is unnecessary.
\end{proof}

For an allowed failure fraction $\delta$, define
\[
 R^{\rm grid}_{M,\delta}(\varepsilon)
 =\max\bigl(\{0\}\cup\{r_j:p_j(\varepsilon)\le\delta\}\bigr),\qquad
 \widehat R_{M,\delta}(\varepsilon)
 =\max\bigl(\{0\}\cup\{r_j:\overline p_j\le\delta\}\bigr).
\]
On the simultaneous event, $\widehat R_{M,\delta}\le R^{\rm grid}_{M,\delta}$, including when the radius is selected after viewing the scan. The guarantee concerns random directions in the fixed space; it does not assert preservation along every direction or on unseen data.
