# Contribution framing agreed with Giulia

Recorded 2026-11-19 after rereading the main paper and appendices. Read this before advising on the paper, drafting reviewer responses, or interpreting new application results.

The primary contribution is the discovery and constructive characterization of large, high-dimensional convex regions of exact or approximate functional invariance on a dataset at finite, non-infinitesimal parameter distances. The question is which parameter edits can vary independently and simultaneously while preserving the network outputs throughout a region. A Jacobian/GGN kernel alone describes only first-order invisibility; a tangent to a curved symmetry orbit alone does not supply finite straight edits or their convex combinations.

The exact response decomposition is a major contribution in its own right. The frozen polynomial retains all mixed-weight interactions, while the activation-transition remainder isolates activation nonlinearity. This enables the atomic decomposition, efficient exact multilayer compatibility test, constructive invariant spaces, dimension results, and the analysis of true-network extent. It also provides a tool for studying neural networks beyond oceans.

Present the work in this order: finite convex functional freedom; its mechanisms and constructive characterization; its size, extent, emergence and persistence during learning; implications and applications. Hessian coverage and LMC are applications, not the standard by which the central contribution succeeds or fails. Do not recenter the paper on those applications or demand a successful CL algorithm to establish the geometric contribution.

Keep mathematical scope precise without burying the finding: feature-null spaces give arbitrary-amplitude true invariance; general frozen invariant spaces require activation-region control or residual bounds for true-network guarantees. Sampled large radii are empirical extent measurements, not uniform ball certificates. The relevant volume is within the affine hull; lower-dimensional oceans also have zero ambient volume. Prior work includes affine invariances as well as curved symmetries, so articulate the specific advance without claiming that all earlier invariant sets were curved.

Practical motivation includes finite constrained updates and sampling that preserve existing predictions. Better continual-learning algorithms or posterior approximations are motivations unless directly demonstrated. The scientific discovery, construction, and decomposition remain valuable independently of those downstream benchmarks.

Giulia wants concise, direct writing that preserves this contribution hierarchy. New experiment integration remains open until explicitly requested. Do not edit the manuscript while working on rebuttal drafts.
